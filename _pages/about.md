I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at <span class="bio-highlight">New York University</span>, working under the supervision of <span class="bio-highlight">Prof. Yuzhang Lin</span>. My research focuses on <span class="bio-highlight">power distribution systems</span>, low-voltage network visibility, smart metering, graph analytics, and learning-based voltage estimation.

Before joining NYU, I completed my undergraduate studies in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at <span class="bio-highlight">Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology</span>. During that time, I worked under the guidance of <span class="bio-highlight">Dr. Abdul Hasib Chowdhury</span> on fault detection in transmission-line power-system networks, where I explored convolutional neural networks and computer vision methods for power-system monitoring.

My current research investigates how sparse high-reporting-rate smart meters can be combined with existing advanced metering infrastructure to improve near-real-time voltage visibility in low-voltage distribution networks. I am particularly interested in <span class="bio-highlight">strategic sensor placement</span>, <span class="bio-highlight">community detection</span>, <span class="bio-highlight">graph neural networks</span>, and data-driven methods that can support reliable and intelligent operation of modern distribution grids.
